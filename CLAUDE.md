## Project Overview

This is a Chinese web novel (网络小说) creation project targeting the **Tomato Novel (番茄小说)** platform. The repository contains planning documents and outlines for a 200-chapter novel.

**Title:** 《摸鱼：我的小说首富成真了》

**Core Premise:** A 28-year-old backend developer named Chen Mo (陈默) discovers that whatever he writes in his novel on a writing platform becomes reality in the real world. He begins using this "keyboard god" ability to climb from a struggling programmer to the world's richest person — while trying to keep his identity secret.

## Novel Architecture

### Four-Act Structure (200 chapters)

| Act | Chapters | Arc |
|-----|----------|-----|
| 觉醒摸鱼 (Awakening) | 1–30 | Discovery of power, small-scale reality edits |
| 暗流涌动 (Undercurrents) | 31–90 | Silent acquisition of company shares, capital battles |
| 王者降临 (King Ascendant) | 91–150 | Identity semi-revealed, global富豪榜, national-level attention |
| 键盘上帝 (Keyboard God) | 151–200 | Source of power revealed, ultimate choice, apotheosis |

### Emotional Design System

The novel follows a strict **"压-小扬-压-爆"** (pressure → small relief → pressure → explosion) rhythm within every 3-chapter unit. Each chapter (~2000 words) contains ~7 emotional inflection points at fixed word-count intervals.

### Core Constraints (Power System Rules)

- Cannot directly rewrite death
- Cannot change major historical events already occurred
- 3–24 hour delay between writing and reality manifestation
- Every ~15 chapters requires an "ability失控" (loss of control) incident

### Suspense Thread Tracking

8 major suspense threads (S-001 through S-008) are seeded and resolved across specific chapters — see the 悬念线追踪表 in the master document.

## Working Conventions

- All novel content and planning lives in the master markdown file
- Chapters are planned at ~2000 words each
- Each chapter title must include a "爽点预告" (spoiler hook)
- Every chapter ending must include a cliffhanger pointing to the next chapter's payoff
- The target demographic is 25–35 year old male office workers seeking escapist wish-fulfillment
